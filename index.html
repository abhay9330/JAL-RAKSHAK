ABOUT JAL RAKSHAK

Technology With a Purpose.

Jal Rakshak is a water-conservation project concept designed to make reporting water leakage easier. It provides a simple interface where citizens can share leakage details, upload supporting photographs, and identify the location of the problem.

Our goal is to encourage faster identification of water wastage and promote awareness about the importance of conserving water.

Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information about Jal Rakshak.

What is Jal Rakshak?

Jal Rakshak is a project concept that provides an interface for reporting water leakage and promoting water conservation.

How can I report a leakage?

Complete the reporting form, enter the location and issue details, upload a photo, and press Submit Complaint.

What kind of photograph can I upload?

The form accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP image formats. The selected file is previewed in the browser, but the actual image file is not uploaded to the database in this version.

Is my complaint automatically sent to an authority?

No. Complaint details are saved in the connected project database, but they are not automatically forwarded to a government department or other authority.

How can I track my complaint?

After submitting a complaint, keep the reference number displayed on the screen. Enter that number in the Track Complaint section to check its currently recorded status.

Can I use Jal Rakshak on a mobile phone?

Yes. The website is intended to have a responsive layout for mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
